Early Prediction of Alzheimer’s Disease and Conversion

Leakage-free, participant-level machine-learning analysis of multimodal ADNI data. Conversion is defined as a worse diagnostic stage sustained across two consecutive visits.

Methods, in brief. All models are participant-level (one baseline visit per person predicting the future outcome), evaluated with 5-fold stratified cross-validation and SMOTE applied inside training folds only. Cohorts: CN→progression n=519, MCI→Dementia n=819, Pooled→AD n=1338. The corrupted PTAU variable (a duplicate of TAU) was excluded; 32 predictors were used.
